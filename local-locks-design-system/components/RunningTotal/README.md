# RunningTotal and LongVisitNotice

**RunningTotal** is what the visit adds up to so far: "Estimated total" (Cormorant Garamond, 1.6rem) on the left and "Total time" on the right, with a line of small print under. It is a `white` bar with a `gold-soft` border, and it is a polite live region, so a screen reader hears the new total as services are added.

- On the Book page, before she has picked a stylist, the price is a range across the stylists who do every service picked ("$140-190+"), or one price when only one does. With nobody offering the combination it shows a dash and says so.
- While booking with one stylist it is her exact starting total ("$270+").
- It sticks to the bottom of the screen while she keeps choosing and rests above the main action at the end of the page. The review step repeats the total, larger, above Confirm booking.
- Prices keep their plus: they are always starting prices.

**LongVisitNotice** is a `Notice` shown when a visit is longer than four hours: "That's a long visit. Everything you picked adds up to about 4 hr 30 min. You may want to set aside the afternoon, or book some of it on another day. Either is fine." It is a heads-up, never a warning: no alarm color, no pressure words.
